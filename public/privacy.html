KillMyStartup

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 August 2026

KillMyStartup is a voice application. You pitch a startup idea out loud, and an AI searches the web and argues back. This policy explains exactly what happens to what you say. It applies to the Android app and to killmystartup.today.

What we collect

  • Voice recordings. While a session is active, your microphone audio is streamed to our voice provider so the AI can hear and respond to you.
  • Transcripts. Your speech is converted to text, along with the AI's replies, to carry on the conversation.
  • Your search terms. Topics drawn from your pitch are sent to a web-search provider to find competitors and market information.
  • Trial usage. A running total of the free-trial seconds you have used is stored locally on your own device. It never leaves the device.

We do not ask for an account, a name, an email address, or a phone number. We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, or files. We do not use analytics or advertising trackers.

When we collect it

The microphone is used only while a session is running — after you press the button to start, and until you press it again, the trial expires, or the session times out. It is never used in the background and never while the app is closed.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your data. We share it only with the providers that make the app work:

  • ElevenLabs — processes your voice audio and transcripts to power the conversation. See elevenlabs.io/privacy.
  • Firecrawl — receives search queries derived from your pitch to retrieve public web results. See firecrawl.dev/privacy.
  • Vercel — hosts the website and the small server function that starts a session. See vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy.

How long we keep it

Conversation audio and transcripts are retained by our voice provider, ElevenLabs, for as long as the app account exists, so that sessions can be reviewed for quality and debugging. We have not set an automatic deletion period. You can ask us to delete your recordings at any time — see below.

The trial counter stored on your device is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

Deleting your data

Email contact@killmystartup.today and ask us to delete your conversation data, or follow the step-by-step instructions on our data deletion page. Tell us roughly when you used the app so we can find the right sessions. We will delete them and confirm by reply. You may also clear the app's storage from Android Settings to remove everything held on your device.

Security

All traffic between the app and our providers is encrypted in transit over HTTPS. Our API credentials are held on the server and are never shipped inside the app.

Children

KillMyStartup is not directed at children and is not intended for anyone under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has used the app, contact us and we will delete the data.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date at the top of this page. Continuing to use the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

For questions, deletion requests, or complaints, email contact@killmystartup.today.
